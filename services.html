A Special Inspection is a third-party verification process required by the NYC Department of Buildings (DOB) to ensure that specific structural and safety-critical construction work is performed in accordance with approved plans and building codes. It is typically required for structural steel connections, high-strength concrete, masonry, excavation, fire-stopping, and more. BR Civil LLC is certified to perform all DOB-required Special Inspections across NYC.

Resident Engineering Inspection (REI) involves placing a qualified engineer on-site full-time or part-time to oversee construction activities. This includes reviewing contractor work quality, verifying material compliance, logging daily site conditions, coordinating with architects and owners, and ensuring the project progresses in alignment with contract documents and regulatory requirements. BR Civil LLC provides dedicated REI services for commercial, infrastructure, and civil projects across Queens and NYC.

Our cost estimation process uses detailed quantity take-offs from architectural and engineering drawings, current NYC market pricing data, and verified subcontractor rate benchmarks. We typically deliver estimates within ±5–10% accuracy depending on design completeness. We also provide value engineering recommendations that can significantly reduce project costs without compromising structural integrity or compliance.
