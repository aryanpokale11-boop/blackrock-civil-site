Our Capabilities

One team.
Five core disciplines.

Every civil project is unique. Our engineering capabilities can be tailored to the size, complexity, timeline, and NYC compliance mandates of your site.

01

REI (Resident Engineering Inspection)

Full-time on-site technical inspection, material verification, contractor coordination, and progress log auditing.

02

Cost Estimation

Detailed quantity take-offs, trade cost breakdowns, pre-construction feasibility audits, and value engineering reviews.

03

Special Inspections

NYC DOB-compliant inspections covering structural steel, concrete, masonry, excavation, and life-safety systems.

04

Plumbing Engineering

Civil plumbing schematics, municipal water main hookups, backflow prevention, and installation code verification.

05

Construction Management

End-to-end site supervision, subcontractor alignment, safety protocol enforcement, and timely handover execution.
